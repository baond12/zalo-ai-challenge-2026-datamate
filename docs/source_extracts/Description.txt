0001: Spreadsheets and simple databases are the most widely used data systems in the world. A shop owner keeps sales and inventory in Excel; a teacher keeps grades and attendance; a clinic keeps patient visits and medicine batches; an HR officer keeps timesheets; a cooperative keeps yields and input costs; a commune office keeps household statistics; a journalist downloads open data from the General Statistics Office; an employee of a large company wants a number from the data warehouse without waiting for the data team. All of them have the data. Almost none of them can write a query. What they want is to ask in plain Vietnamese — “Tháng này bán chạy nhất mặt hàng gì?”, “Tuần rồi bán được bao nhiêu ly trà sữa?”, “Còn ai nợ tiền chưa trả?”, “Thuốc nào sắp hết hạn trong kho?” — and get a correct answer.

0005: DataMate challenges you to build an AI agent that answers natural-language Vietnamese questions directly on structured data (CSV, Excel, SQLite): given the raw files and a question, the agent returns a correct answer — or declines to answer when the data cannot support the question. The competition data is anchored in the world of Vietnamese small businesses — the largest group of spreadsheet users in the country and the one with the richest, messiest real-world data — but the capability you build is general: natural-language question answering over tabular data.
0006: 
0007:  
0008: 
0009: Input:
0010: 
0011:  • A data package for one business: one or more files (.csv, .xlsx, .sqlite) plus a short README.md describing the business.
0012: 
0013:  • A question in everyday Vietnamese.
0014: 
0015:  • An as-of date: the “today” against which relative time expressions (“tháng này”, “tuần rồi”, “cùng kỳ năm ngoái”) are resolved.
0016: 
0017:  • A rulebook and a solar–lunar calendar, shared by every business of the split (see the Data section)).
0018: 
0019:  
0020: 
0021: Output:
0022: 
0023:  • A final answer of one of the types in the Evaluation section: a value (number, percent, string, date, boolean, list, table), unanswerable when the data cannot support the question, or abstain when the agent chooses not to answer.

0029: Input — one line of questions.jsonl:

0032:
